I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://www.hkust.edu.hk/), advised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Prof. Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
